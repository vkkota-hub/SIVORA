# SEO and content release checklist

## Important
No agency or implementation can guarantee a “100%” Google ranking. Rankings depend on competition, authority, backlinks, user intent, geography, content quality, technical performance and ongoing maintenance. The objective is to remove preventable technical/content weaknesses and build authority over time.

## Before launch
- Replace `sivora.example` in canonical tags, sitemap and structured data with the production domain.
- Confirm legal company name, real office address, phone and monitored email.
- Add Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and Accessibility Statement.
- Configure analytics only after consent requirements are decided.
- Configure Search Console and submit sitemap.
- Verify redirects and only one canonical host (www or non-www).
- Generate per-page title/description, OG image and canonical metadata.
- Test Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals on mobile and desktop.
- Compress images and lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Add real author/date metadata to Insights.
- Do not publish placeholder articles just to create volume.

## Content quality rules
- No spun competitor copy.
- No duplicate service pages with only city/country names changed.
- No fake statistics or unnamed “leading global bank” case studies unless legally approved and true.
- Use specific problems, project patterns, constraints, architecture decisions and lessons learned.
- Prefer 700 useful words over 2,000 generic words.
- Have a real subject-matter expert review every technical article.

## High-value content opportunities
- Cloud modernisation decision guides
- Data platform / data quality architecture patterns
- AI readiness and governance checklists
- Application modernisation assessment framework
- Engineering delivery operating model articles
- Regulated-industry architecture considerations
- Practical migration and platform case studies once real client approvals exist
